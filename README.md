# Ventline

Communication app for HVAC companies — a direct line between field technicians and managers.

## The idea

HVAC field crews and office managers lose time and information in scattered phone calls, texts, and paper notes. Ventline gives them one shared channel:

- **Job updates** — technicians report status, photos, and issues from the field
- **Dispatch & assignments** — managers assign jobs and see who's where
- **Messaging** — job-scoped threads instead of buried group chats
- **Handoffs** — shift notes and open items that don't get lost
